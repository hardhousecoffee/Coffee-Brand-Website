# Espresso-machine photography: source review

Research date: October 30, 2026.

## Outcome

This investigation did not establish a replacement that meets **all** the owner's conditions: exact model, premium uncluttered lifestyle photography, and verified permission for Hard House Coffee's intended reuse. No website photographs, article text, metadata, purchasing links, or layouts were changed. No applications were submitted, accounts created, or permission requests sent.

This is a record of inspected sources, not a claim that manufacturers never license their photographs. Approved affiliate creatives and public press resources do exist. Access to a photograph is not itself evidence that its licence covers this website.

## Official sources and specific obstacles

### Breville / Sage

- Official free affiliate program: https://www.sageappliances.com/en-gb/learn-more/sage-affiliates-program
- The page explicitly offers a specific range of creative for onsite use and routes applications through Impact. The published program is Sage/UK; US Breville eligibility and the particular image rights still need confirmation.
- Official page distinguishes affiliate enquiries from PR/content-creator enquiries. A request for editorial images does not require reactivating Amazon or the suspended Shop.
- Public Australian media room: https://www.breville.com/en-au/learn-more/media-room
- Inspected its linked release index. It lists older releases, including the Infuser and Dual Boiler; it does not supply the requested Bambino, Bambino Plus, and Barista Express image package.
- Actual-model product galleries located:
  - Bambino BES450: https://www.breville.com/en-us/product/bes450
  - Bambino Plus BES500: https://www.breville.com/en-us/product/bes500
  - Barista Express BES870: https://www.breville.com/en-us/product/bes870
- These galleries identify manufacturer assets, but no unrestricted licence for this site's republication was established.
- General terms reviewed: https://www.breville.com/en-us/legal/terms-of-use-and-sale
- Best next route: obtain approval for specified editorial photographs, or use the exact creatives supplied under an approved applicable affiliate agreement. Do not assume ordinary gallery assets are included in an affiliate grant.

### De'Longhi

- Official corporate press kit: https://www.delonghigroup.com/en/media/press-kit
- Actual image download: https://www.delonghigroup.com/en/foto
- Downloaded the linked approximately 39 MB archive temporarily and inspected its file listing. It contains group logos, headquarters photographs, and executive portraits—not Stilosa machine photographs. It cannot solve the Stilosa requirement.
- Actual brand asset library: https://library.delonghigroup.com/assetbank-delonghi/
- Library presents employee SSO and external-user login. External registration exists, requiring identity/organization information and a CAPTCHA. No registration was submitted and no gated image rights were inferred.
- Official affiliate information: https://www.delonghi.com/en-gb/e/affiliate-program-signup
- Explicitly states that applicants are reviewed, and **once approved** receive a dashboard with professional banner creatives and a product data feed. This is a real promotional route, but is not immediate permission to reuse every public product photograph.
- Best next route: approved external media-library access or an editorial image release specifically covering Stilosa EC260/BK. Do not substitute Dedica, Stilosa EC230, or La Specialista.

### Seattle Coffee Gear

- Official Media Partner Kit: https://www.seattlecoffeegear.com/pages/media-kit
- Inspected page, its affiliate application destination, media contact, and linked media-kit PDF destination.
- Provides an Impact affiliate application, influencer proposal form, and a marketing contact. The page does not establish a general licence to republish all retailer product photographs.
- Best next route: request an exact-model editorial image pack with permission, or obtain approved affiliate creatives. Do not add retailer purchase links while the Shop suspension remains in force.

### Rancilio

- Best next route: request the identified Silvia Pro X lifestyle files and editorial permission. Do not substitute the earlier Silvia Pro or ordinary Silvia.

### La Marzocco

- Located an actual public press-kit page rather than only the consumer product gallery:
  https://www.lamarzocco.com/it/en/welcome-to-casa-la-marzocco-the-design-experience/
- The page links English/Italian releases and a Microsoft SharePoint marketing archive explicitly offered as its Milan Design Week media gallery.
- The shared archive's top-level contents were readable: BRAND ASSETS, PHOTOS & VIDEOS, and a digital agenda.
- The attempted nested-folder URL returned the same top-level listing; individual suitable Micra photographs and their usage instructions were not verified. Do not report that the entire archive is inaccessible or requires login merely because a Sign in link is displayed.
- This is event-specific material, not a verified blanket release for all Linea Micra product photography.
- Best next route: obtain the exact Micra files and applicable editorial/credit instructions through the published press resource.

### Slayer / Cimbali Group

- Official current Espresso V3 product destination located: https://slayerespresso.com/product/espresso-v3/
- Official contact: https://slayerespresso.com/contact/
- Also inspected the manufacturer's historical photography post:
  https://slayerespresso.com/slayer-machine-pictures/
- That post describes a **2008 pre-production machine**, links legacy image-host URLs, and is not evidence for a contemporary Espresso 2-Group product photograph or an applicable reuse licence.
- Public blog photography and parent-group promotional material did not establish a usable exact-model editorial image grant.
- Best next route: request current Espresso 2-Group/V3 lifestyle press photographs and reuse/credit terms from the manufacturer.

### Kees van der Westen

- Official Spirit gallery: https://keesvanderwesten.com/spirit/
- Contains attractive actual-model café photographs, including credited professional work. Credit alone does not establish republication permission.
- Best next route: request the specified Spirit photograph with the photographer/manufacturer's applicable editorial release.

## Existing/free candidates deliberately not installed

- An existing uploaded Encore ESP/Bambino photograph was inspected. The grinder dominates, the machine is soft-focused, and the kitchen is not the premium environment requested. Its presence in uploads does not independently document its reuse rights.
- Previously sourced Unsplash Barista Express photographs and CC BY 2.0 café photographs of Spirit/Slayer were visually inspected. They do not meet the current requested visual standard; the Slayer rear view also does not verify the required group configuration.
- Existing catalogue machines must not stand in for different models merely because their images are attractive.

## Outstanding website corrections

The priority $20,000 and $300 article images, missing Bambino Plus/Stilosa/Barista Express profile photographs, and remaining model/image mismatches are **not fixed by this research**. A verified suitable asset is still required before installation. No claim of completed visual testing is made because no application changes were made.

The lowest-cost remaining route is a free, specific editorial image request through the identified manufacturer/retailer contacts—not buying photographs, joining paid services, inventing product images, or reactivating purchasing links.
