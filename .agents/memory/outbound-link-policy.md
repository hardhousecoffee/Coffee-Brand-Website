name: Outbound article references and protected business links
description: Owner's visitor-retention policy distinguishes non-affiliate references from protected business, social, video and functional links.
---

Non-affiliate third-party article references may be made non-clickable only within the inventory the owner has approved. Preserve source names, destination information, factual text, research notes, product details, photographs and formatting.

**Why:** The owner explicitly treats this as a visitor-retention and business strategy, not permission to erase research or citations.

**How to apply:** Change the approved references themselves, not a global link handler. Do not extend a cleanup to additional destinations without approval. If ownership, purpose or business benefit is uncertain, preserve the link and report it.

Official Hard House Coffee social accounts, all existing YouTube links/players/thumbnails/playback, internal links, authorized affiliates and merchandise/Printify links are intentional exceptions. Keep the Amazon Shop suspension intact.

**Why:** Social and video engagement support the owner's brand and business even when visitors leave the domain. The owner explicitly forbids changing these features during article-reference cleanup.

**How to apply:** Preserve existing link destinations and embed settings exactly. Do not modify contact submissions, fonts, analytics, advertising, consent, legal pages, hosting, prerendering, sitemap or responsive design as part of hyperlink cleanup.
