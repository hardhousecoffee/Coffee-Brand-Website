---
name: Blog expansion editorial boundaries
description: Owner-approved development-only scope, distinct topic selection and evidence requirements for blog work.
---

Blog expansion must preserve the homepage, navigation, footer, branding, hosting setup, existing article URLs and unrelated catalog/assets. Publication requires separate explicit owner approval.

**Why:** The owner explicitly limited this work to the existing blog and associated article SEO, while retaining the reversible Amazon Shop suspension.

**How to apply:** Extend the current article/category system rather than adding top-level content surfaces or redesigning the site. Do not treat development authorization as permission to push or publish.

The owner has reviewed and approved the twelve expansion articles for retention. Refine them in place rather than replacing them or starting over.

**Why:** The owner explicitly approved the articles and requested a final editorial polish, not a new writing project.

**How to apply:** Preserve their subjects, factual references and existing integrations during editorial work; avoid repeating the research/no-hands-on disclosure throughout each article.

Research-based articles must not claim ownership, hands-on testing, unverifiable ratings, current stock, recent advertising approval dates or invented search-demand figures. Distinct articles need distinct reader intent; a product mention is different from a dedicated review.

**Why:** The owner wants original useful expansion rather than duplicate reviews or filler, and explicitly prohibits fabricated evidence.

**How to apply:** Inventory existing topics and catalog records before selecting future topics; use primary references with clear commercial-source limits, retain images only with accurate subject/model descriptions, and distinguish observable advertising code from verified monetization.

Hard House Coffee is a consumer coffee blog, not a technical training website. Its audience includes everyday readers with different levels of coffee knowledge who may arrive from search or social media.

**Why:** The owner explicitly asked for engaging, approachable magazine-style buying guides rather than instruction manuals or dry product catalogs.

**How to apply:** Use short paragraphs, visible prices, accurate photographs, easy-to-find finishes and practical buying tradeoffs. Keep technical details only when they clarify a decision; condense educational advice and link to existing deeper guides instead of explaining machine operation step by step.

Owner-supplied article and shopping-screenshot prices are reference prices, not guarantees of current offers or instructions to overwrite more recently verified prices.

**Why:** The owner explicitly distinguished supplied reference prices from verified current pricing in both the five-machine and eight-machine guides.

**How to apply:** Preserve the finished supplied wording while keeping displayed prices, exact model identifiers and photograph associations tied to the verified product records. Do not treat a copy replacement as fresh pricing research.

The standalone Linea Micra article was intentionally retired in favor of the eight-machine comparison under $3,000. Its archived source is not a request to restore it.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested removal from blog discovery and the sitemap, with the old address redirecting to the replacement comparison.

**How to apply:** Preserve the redirect and do not reintroduce the standalone listing without approval. This does not prohibit discussing the Linea Micra within other relevant comparisons.

Espresso-machine photography must show the actual model in professional, uncluttered lifestyle settings. Do not substitute attractive photographs of another model, plain-background cutouts, amateur snapshots, or generated product likenesses.

**Why:** The owner repeatedly rejected unsuitable photography and explicitly requested magazine-quality images with verified free reuse rights.

**How to apply:** Investigate manufacturer press galleries, media kits, affiliate creative libraries, authorized retailer resources, and existing assets—not only stock-photo sites. Check the actual download contents and applicable permissions before calling a source usable or unavailable. Report precise access or licensing obstacles rather than repeating a general copyright warning.

The luxury espresso feature intentionally covers both commercial café machines and luxury home machines; keep those applications distinct rather than treating all expensive machines as interchangeable home upgrades.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested four machines in each application, with configuration-specific specifications, pricing and buyer guidance.

**How to apply:** Distinguish group count, utilities, service requirements and optional dosing/profiling equipment. A GS3 MP price must not be replaced by an AV/base-model price, and custom finishes pictured are not automatically included.
